## 🌎 About GEODES

Welcome to the **GEODES Laboratory** — **GEOmatics Dedicated to Exploration of Space**.

The GEODES Laboratory is based at the **Université de Sherbrooke**, Québec, Canada, and is led by **Prof. Myryam Lemelin**, Canada Research Chair in Northern and Planetary Geological Remote Sensing.

Our research focuses on the use of **remote sensing**, **geomatics**, and **planetary analog studies** to better understand the geology and surface properties of Earth, the Moon, Mars, and small planetary bodies.

---

## 🚀 Research Focus

The GEODES Laboratory investigates the surface properties of planetary and terrestrial environments in support of space exploration missions.

Our main research interests include:

- 🛰️ Remote sensing of planetary surfaces  
- 🌕 Lunar geology and regolith properties  
- 🔴 Martian surface composition and geological analogues  
- ☄️ Asteroids and small planetary bodies  
- ❄️ Ice and cryospheric environments  
- 🧭 Geomatics applied to space exploration  
- 🏔️ Canadian Arctic geology as an analogue for Mars  
- 🌡️ Arctic environments in the context of climate change  

---

## 🧪 Planetary Analogue Research

One of the laboratory’s major research directions is the study of the **Canadian Arctic** as an analogue environment for Mars.

Through projects such as **T-MARS** — *Terrestrial Mineral Analysis by Remote Sensing* — the team studies the composition of Arctic gossans and other geological formations that may be comparable to environments found on Mars.

These analogue studies help improve our understanding of planetary surface processes and support the preparation and interpretation of future exploration missions.
